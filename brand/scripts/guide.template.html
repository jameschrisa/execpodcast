Overview

Executive Upskill is a live video podcast. Three operators argue one AI decision at a time from the brand, finance and venture seats, live on YouTube, with clips on YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. This page is the brand reference for people and for agents. The same facts sit in brand.json and in the JSON block on the Agents tab.

Positioning

Campaign line. One question. Three lenses.

Hero line. Three operators talk AI, business and brand from the seats they've held. Live on YouTube, clipped for your feed.

One sentence. Three operators argue the AI decision you are facing from the brand, finance and venture seats, live, and you can ask them in the chat.

Audience

Executives and operators who have to make decisions about AI and want to hear them argued from more than one seat: marketing and brand leaders, finance leaders, founders and investors. Guests are founders and operators who made a hard call on AI, money or brand and will defend it on camera.

What makes it different

  • The hosts argue. Each makes his own call on the same question, and they disagree on camera.
  • They speak from seats they have held: a marketing executive with ML patents, a finance executive of about 20 years, a fund Co-GP who runs the fund's operations on AI agents he designed.
  • Viewers ask live. Questions that name a real decision get picked.
  • One argument per clip, short enough for the gap between meetings.

Logo

The logo is a square: EXECUTIVE in off-white over UPSKILL in orange, both stretched to the same width inside a near-black square. It is the site mark, the home-screen icon and the basis for the favicon. Every file is outlined paths, so no font is needed.

{{LOGO_TILES}}

Clear space

Keep at least 9% of the mark's width (the square's own padding, 9 of 100 units) free on every side. For the wordmark, keep clear space equal to the cap height of the letter L. These are working rules derived from the mark's built-in padding.

{{LOGO_MARK_SMALL}}

Minimum size

  • Square mark: 32 px wide on screen, 12 mm in print.
  • Below 48 px use the monogram, as the site does for the favicon.
  • Wordmark: 120 px wide.

Dark surfaces

On #0e0e0d to #171716 the black square meets near-black. Draw a 2-unit stroke of white at 16% opacity (rgb(255 255 255 / 0.16)) around the square. The SVG files do not include it.

Do

  • Place the mark on a quiet surface with its clear space.
  • Use the mono white file over dark or photographic areas and mono black for one-color print.
  • Use #ff4d12 for UPSKILL on the mark, and #ff5a1f only in the dark wordmark.

Do not

  • Re-set the words in another font or let the two lines differ in width.
  • Change colors, swap which word is orange, or put orange on a similar orange.
  • Add shadows, gradients, outlines or rounded corners. The square has sharp corners.
  • Rotate, skew, tilt or stretch the square into a rectangle.
  • Place mono white on light backgrounds or mono black on dark ones.

Color

Light is the default theme. Dark applies only when asked for. The palette is off-white paper, off-black ink and one accent, the "on-air" orange. Each swatch shows light on the left and dark on the right, with its WCAG contrast against that theme's paper.

Visual language

Shapes

Cards and panels have a 16px radius. Buttons and chips are full pills. Inputs are 12px. Nothing else. Focus outlines use a 6px radius. Avatars are circles with a 2px ring at 20% of the current color.

Buttons

Four pills: primary (accent fill, on-accent text, turns ink on hover), dark (ink fill, turns accent on hover), outline (1px ink border, fills ink on hover) and text (no fill, underlined). One filled button per area.

Lens panels

A 16px card with an inset printed-card border: a 6px inner band in the panel color, then a 2px ring in the ring color. Display title, host avatar chip, a linocut print rotated -6 degrees (straightens on hover), and episodes divided by hairlines in the current text color at 15% opacity.

Grain

A film grain over the page: SVG fractal noise, baseFrequency 0.9, 2 octaves, 160px tile, 5% opacity in light and 7% in dark, non-interactive. The banners and the title and end cards use it. Share card, email header, lower thirds and slides stay flat.

Illustration

Single-color linocut prints traced to SVG, drawn with fill="currentColor" so they take the text color of the panel: megaphone (Brand), adding machine (Money), knight (Venture), microphone. Keep them adult. The icon set matches them: megaphone, calculator, chess-knight.

Photography

Candid home-studio frames of the hosts: three-quarter at the mic, a laughing side profile, square face crops for avatars. Host cards are 3:4 with a 16px radius and a scrim gradient rising over the lower 64%. Greg's photos are generated until real ones arrive.

Layout habits

Content container max 1400px with 16, 24 and 32px side padding by breakpoint. Sections start 96 to 128px apart. A section opens with a hairline row, then a large uppercase display headline, then a lede. Scroll padding is 88px under a sticky nav.

Surfaces

Web, email and slides default to light paper. Social profile art, banners and video cards sit on the logo black (#0e0e0e) with off-white and orange. The share card uses palette colors only.

Voice and messaging

Direct and specific, spoken by people who have done the job. It asks a pointed question, gives a concrete test and skips the pitch. Confident and a little dry in headlines, plain and helpful in forms and FAQ, never hype.

Rules

  • No dashes. Never write an em dash, an en dash or a horizontal bar (U+2014, U+2013, U+2015). Use a comma, period, colon, parentheses or a rewrite. Numeric ranges take a hyphen (0-4).
  • No banned terms. The hard-ban list is in ~/.claude/standards/anti-ai-banned.txt and in brand.json under voice.bannedTerms. It covers stock transitions, copula dodges, buzzwords, brochure tone and filler openers and closers. Name the person, the number or the decision instead of a vague claim about AI "transforming" things.
  • No antithesis frames, no semicolons in copy, no hedging, no recap closers.
  • No eyebrow labels above headings. A label stays only when it carries data: a status, a category, a unit.
  • Vary sentence length. Contractions are normal ("you're", "we'll").
  • The lens-test takes are drafts each host must approve. Quote them with care. Episode titles are working titles.
